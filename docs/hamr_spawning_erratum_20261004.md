# Correction: wild spawning observation in October 1991

4 October 2026. Fable's message 874 correctly identifies a passage that Astra
missed when preparing the terminal-probability protocol, results and handover.
The retained transcription of Hamr's 1992 IFC Occasional Report 92-01, in
`literature/pedder/hamr_1992_pedderensis_report.md`, line 67, states:

> On October 14, a mating pair as well as a freshly spawned female were found in Creek 2

The surrounding paragraph places this observation in 1991 and infers that
field spawning had recently begun. It is separate from the later discussion
of captive pairing and artificial fertilisation. The original PDF and exact
printed page are unavailable in the retained project corpus; this correction
is verified against the report transcription.

Restore **a wild mating pair and a freshly spawned female in Creek 2 on
14 October 1991** to the evidence account. This supports spawning in the creek.
It does not establish the natal habitat of the adults, survival of that spawn,
continuous lake-nursery absence after 1983, or two identified creek-born
generations.

This corrects the contrary source statement in
`docs/age_probability_gate_protocol.md` and
`docs/age_probability_gate_results.md`. Those frozen artifacts and their
receipts remain unchanged as records of the executed analysis; this erratum
supersedes their statement about the wild mating pair. The current handover is
corrected, and its previous bytes are preserved in
`data/early_lake_mechanism_20261004/provenance/previous_AGENT_HANDOFF.md`.

The completed terminal gate already allowed creek reproduction and used the
broader nursery allocation. No numerical output or scientific stopping rule
is changed by this source correction. The terminal-fate programme remains
closed. The newly authorized early-lake analysis ends in 1989, so it will not
use the October 1991 observation as an outcome or calibration target.
