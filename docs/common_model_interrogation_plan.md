# Existing-artifact ecological interrogation

20 September 2026. Barry authorised this tranche using Twilight Laboratory,
before re-engaging Fable. This is an exploratory description of frozen results,
not a new calibration, holdout test or revision of the signed comparison.

## Endpoint and boundaries

Deliver an ecological account of contrasting supported histories and the
available early-lake overshoot contrasts, with figures and a bounded independent
check. Preserve the existing likelihood, q scenarios, source selection, capacity
labels, MC criteria and factual counterfactual weights. No simulations, replay,
new detection bound, new mechanism, combined arm, commit, push or publication.
Do not seek a new Fable review until the analysis is complete and reported here.

## Descriptive selection and estimands

1. The main descriptive pool is the already-reported permissive primary slice:
   early lower95 >= .50, joint lower95 >= 1e-6, and qualified joint weights.
   Keep all other cutoff cells in the original map. This slice is not newly
   promoted to an empirical adequacy standard.
2. For comparable illustrations, fix base configuration, q=.02 and shared
   quality. In each restriction choose the minimum, middle (lower median rank),
   and maximum weighted-survival source, ordered by survival then source ID.
   For ordering only, clip to [0,1] and round to 12 decimal places so numerical
   residues around probability one do not determine illustration choices.
   Retain duplicate selection roles if they select the same source. These are
   illustrative order statistics, not posterior representatives or rankings.
3. To expose the strongest loss examples under a common observation law, add
   each restriction's minimum-survival profile across configurations at q=.02,
   shared quality, and both already-qualified q=.20/shared-quality R_C sources.
   Label the changed configurations. Do not impose a persistence/loss cutoff.
4. Report every selected source's annual refuge means and presence probabilities
   from the existing summaries. Individual frozen histories lack full annual
   lake/refuge trajectories. For each selected source choose the largest-factual-
   weight surviving and extinct replicate separately, if each has positive
   support; report its weight and available recorded states. These are examples,
   not typical histories. Do not replay to fill missing states.
5. Across all qualifying profiles, use archived natural-range counts at actual
   state years 1997–2001 (1998–2002 survey proxies) and endpoint 2002. Report
   factual-weighted distributions conditional on survival through 2002, with
   conditional weight concentration. These totals are not refuge-only counts.
   Outcome conditioning is a separate descriptive estimand; it does not change
   original compatibility or renormalise away invalid counterfactuals.
   At endpoint 2002 only, refuge mean conditional on survival is also recoverable
   as its factual-weighted mean divided by weighted survival, since refuge
   abundance is zero on every extinct endpoint. No analogous identity is used
   to condition earlier refuge states on terminal survival. The archived means
   do not supply the covariance needed for a new conditional-refuge MC SE.
6. Overshoot contrasts use all 254 final R_J sources and both primary laws at
   all four q values. Examine lake counts in 1980, 1983, 1987 and 1988, plus
   the whole-period minimum total. Retain factual joint weights and pairing;
   any altered invalid-removal history with positive factual support makes the
   contrast undefined. Report paired MC SEs and the unchanged weight criteria.
   Highlight the original permissive factual-compatible subset without selecting
   on the counterfactual result. Full 1978–1990 lake trajectories are unavailable.

## Evidence and interpretation

Verify consumed archived files against existing manifests, reproduce the selected
sources' original likelihood/ESS/survival summaries, and check central new
calculations independently. Tie examples to existing historical evidence and its
documented limitations. Uncertain population estimates, repeated capture counts,
and Barry's coverage judgment do not become hard exclusion thresholds. Preserve
the distinction between Monte Carlo error, conditional process spread, parameter
source variation, and uncertainty about field observation/abundance scale.

The stopping point is a verified report explaining what the retained examples
require ecologically, which early-lake contrasts are informative, and the single
most useful next scientific decision. Broader compute and manuscript attribution
remain outside this tranche.
