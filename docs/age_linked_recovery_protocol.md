# Linked runner and prospective endpoint-discrimination test

1 October 2026. This executes the runner/recovery tranche accepted in Fable's
thread 31 message 780, under Barry's standing authorization. Candidate 2 remains
the scientific design. This document specifies the executable qualification;
it does not authorize extra search, new biological arms, or publication.

The stopping point is a verified linked runner and a conditional endpoint-
discrimination result, including an explicit GO/REPAIR/STOP disposition before
historical search. A failed or unassessed gate is retained, not filled by new
draws. Review agreement is not empirical validation.

## Parameter worlds and budget

Use the 20 candidate-2 cells, 64 truth sources and 32 independent inference-bank
sources per cell, and four joint process replicates per source. This is 23,040
site-history attempts; at most 960 further site histories are reserved for
controls, within the existing 24,000 allowance. Algebraically invalid draws and
failed removals/transfers are retained without replacement. Charge every planned
attempt conservatively, even if it cannot start or ends early. Unit fixtures
and the separate 8,000-history implementation/target reserve are reported
separately. The total future allocation stays 1,035,520; no unused allowance
becomes extra search authority.

Half the sources target each side of R0(1-p)=1. In the above-one stratum draw p
uniformly over its reachable support, ending below min(p_max,0.95), then log-R0
uniformly between max(1.2,1/(1-p)) and 20. Below one, draw p over the full
support and log-R0 between 1.2 and min(20,1/(1-p)). This explicitly defined
engineering distribution is not a conditional empirical prior. Report its
strata separately. Ledger timing and March retention are balanced by source
index. Restriction and removal-account comparisons share nuisance draws;
truth/inference roles and parameter/process/observation/bootstrap namespaces
remain disjoint. Reject b>1,200 and unattainable neutral allocations without
replacement. No outcome or historical score selects a source.

The frozen retained forcing table contains PR005, PR007, PR012–034 and PR040.
Use their existing bounded distributions, except the common-protocol uniform
effects PR020/21/27/28/33 and ordered trout pair. Superseded survival, fecundity,
capacity and detection defaults are not imported. The source/forcing/calendar
hashes are bound before execution. The linked runner conserves known capture,
holding, release and donor/recipient age-sex vectors and records failures.

The initial state is the analytic neutral December-1964 state, followed by
annual updates from 1965 through 2012, matching the retained historical scenario.
Round stochastic initial counts by floor(x+0.5); keep deterministic counts
unrounded. Preserve the rounding vector. Retain all invader forcing in every
restriction and apply the already specified one-year threat lag. Compare the
extended equations numerically with the common forcing through 2002.

The executable date endpoints are reconstruction assumptions within the source
windows, not newly recovered dates. The 12 captive adults (5 female, 7 male)
are collected on 1 February 1990 or 27 September 1991; their seven released
survivors comprise 3 female and 4 male. Ten additional adults (5 of each sex)
and one immature fish use 29 August or 27 September 1991. Mature captures have
start-year age at least one, corresponding to attained age at least two at that
year's spawning event; the immature fish has start-age zero. Captures precede
the fixed 1 November release. The reported spawning condition of the 12
broodstock supports adult eligibility; their exact seasonal collection dates
remain unresolved. The wide endpoint bracket is not a reconstruction of
individual visits.

The fixed 4/5 February 1992 capture/release links one adult, plus eleven new
1991-cohort captive offspring of keyed random sex. Stripped parents overlap
the documented adult groups under the existing identity assumption. The primary
additional wild fish is captured on 6 February or 29 November 1992 and released
the following day. Sanger's eleven additional fish are captured at the two
1990/91 endpoints and released with that additional fish, separately from the
twelve released on 5 February. All eleven latent capture ages are conditioned
to remain below the terminal age in 1992; an insufficient eligible source pool
fails without replacement.

January 1996 uses 1/31 January, and the linked three-survivor release uses
1/31 May 1997. For the 12-to-7 and 4-to-3 groups, captured cohorts retain their
ages. Draw known survivors only from release-eligible cohorts, back-map the
selected vector to capture cohorts, and retain its exact complement as the five
or one known captive deaths. Death vectors refer to capture ages; actual death
dates are not invented. Conditioning on known releases adds no holding-survival
likelihood. All date endpoints remain no-op calendar entries in other arms.

Oberon transfers sample all nonterminal ages in both modeled compartments,
because transfer age/sex selectivity is undocumented. Preserve the sampled
compartment-by-sex-by-age donor tensor and add its identical sex-by-age marginal
to Strathgordon. No adult-only or lake-component-only availability gate is used.
Record pre-removal abundance and the removed fraction by event and sex.

## Observation generators and intervention consistency

Synthetic data are newly generated, never the real (68,1,0) vector. The baseline
draws independent Poisson counts with a generator-only common Creek rate chosen
to give expected total 69 over the three blocks. Lake's generator-only rate
gives expected total 173 over the eight retained trips. These information
normalizers do not estimate real catchability or enter candidate scoring.
The fixed-total control instead draws a fresh conditional multinomial Creek
allocation with total 69. The gamma-quality stress uses independent gamma
increments with shape equal to creek-trip effort (scale one), and gamma(1,1)
site-trip multipliers for Lake. Thus fractional K does not require inventing a
fractional integer visit or duplicating a detection record.

Lake uses twelve sites per trip, Poisson counts divided into site means,
Uniform(-0.08,0.08) reading perturbations on nonzero trip means only, and exact
zero means retained. Scores use the generated early/late capture equivalents;
late values may be positive. The reading-endpoint sensitivity shifts nonzero
means by ±0.08, bounded below by zero. Powers are scoring rules, never count
generators. Other records use fresh Poisson counts at the declared q, coarsened
to binary detection. Juvenile proxies and q represent observation scenarios;
they do not turn the published catch shares into counts of the population.
In the stress generator these records receive gamma(1,1) visit factors, with
adult and juvenile outcomes sharing the factor for the same 1997 or 2012 visit.

The known intervention schedule constrains the synthetic records. Define A by:

- early captures at least the modeled removals assigned to the early survey
  window (under the stated common-coverage assumption);
- at least one capture in February 1992;
- at least four captures at the January 1996 collection;
- a March 1996 capture if permanent March retention is one.

Generate source, process and observations first. If A fails, preserve the draw,
counts, terminal label and reason; do not redraw or classify that dataset as
coherent. This is observation-layer rejection, separate from an impossible
demographic removal. It avoids contradictory synthetic evidence while keeping
the reviewed historical scoring functions unchanged. Joint conditioning is
proportional to p(source)p(history|source)p(data|history) I(A); it does not
require dividing the historical likelihood by a candidate-specific truncation
probability. No terminal label enters A. The assessment is **conditional
endpoint-discrimination qualification**, not posterior calibration or exact
recovery of an unknown historical sampling/stopping process.

Report process feasibility and observation coherence separately, by cell,
refuge-index stratum, terminal class and profile. Give source/abundance shifts
after conditioning, not only accuracy on a selected subset. Count rejected
observations as abstentions in an additional coverage-weighted diagnostic.

## Classification, controls and interpretation

For each candidate class, average likelihoods over same-class replicates of
each bank source, then over distinct bank sources. Use equal class reference
weights and a fixed zero log-ratio decision threshold. Require eight distinct
bank sources per class and source-weight ESS≥4 in each class; otherwise abstain.
Use identical bank histories/datasets for Creek-only, Creek+Lake, and full
scores. The feasibility-only score has no class information under these equal
class references and must not discriminate through labels or IDs.

Require sixteen distinct coherent truth sources contributing to each terminal
class before assessing a cell/index-stratum/profile. Partition both truth and
inference banks by the refuge-index stratum before scoring; a pooled result
cannot authorize GO. Compute class sensitivity within each
source and average over sources. Abstentions are errors. Bootstrap truth-source
IDs (1,000 replicates), carrying their process replicates and the paired score
ablations together. Keep the prospective absolute thresholds: balanced accuracy
≥0.75, both sensitivities≥0.70, and lower95% source-bootstrap bound>0.5. Report
paired full-minus-survey improvement and its interval; improvement is not
assumed. The old oracle's 0.54–0.73 values apply to particular trajectories and
are descriptive benchmarks, not universal thresholds.

Informative score controls must discriminate known high/zero exposure; matched
exposure controls with opposite terminal labels must remain indistinguishable.
Runner qualification independently checks actual linked identities, no-removal
empty insurance sites, all-site union calendars, chronological cohort ages,
forcing parity through2002 and exact serial/parallel replay. Controls are
mechanical, not witnesses of an empirical history. Full model and observation
source code remain auditable separately.
Exact feasible pairs with matching observed exposure and opposite endpoints
are not required to pass this population-level gate; their existence remains
open. A pass does not establish individual-history identifiability. Preserve
unweighted small-sex-pool counts in gate histories; factual-weighted shares
belong to the later historical comparison, if authorized by this gate.

Insufficient coherent truth support is unassessed. Insufficient bank support
or concentrated bank weights is a representation failure. With adequate
support, poor classification is a discrimination failure. A pass applies only
to its declared supports, intervention-coherent world distribution and profile;
it does not identify parameters, causal mechanisms, restriction probabilities
or the real historical endpoint. If no primary-panel stratum qualifies, stop
before historical search and report the limiting evidence. A categorical
historical claim is prohibited for an unqualified column.
