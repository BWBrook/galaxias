# Singleton breeding guard: fixed-witness sensitivity

20 September 2026. Completed under Barry's approved amendment and independently
checked. No new search or replacement sources were used.

## Scientific finding

The original witness set is substantially sensitive to the singleton breeding
assumption, but compatible examples remain under all four restrictions. The
amendment therefore narrows the demonstrated compatibility set without resolving
the historical equifinality. It does not establish a ranking of causal accounts,
identify climbing effects, or globally exclude persistence.

Of the 1,527 originally qualified primary source–observation profiles, **600
remain qualified, 193 are Monte Carlo unresolved, and 734 are classified
not-found at the fixed parameter points and thresholds**. The retained profiles
span **226 of 517 original sources**. These are counts within a selected grid,
not posterior probabilities or estimates of the fraction of plausible histories.
The profiles repeat source points across declared detection scenarios and laws.

The guard removes the intended logical defect: across all 529,408 histories,
no zero- or one-fish total population subsequently increased. Nevertheless, the
intervention also changes deterministic initialization and insurance-screen
outcomes. Its effects cannot be attributed solely to late singleton reproduction.

## Experiment and verification

The versioned kernel sets recruitment to zero when pre-breeding
`A_L + A_R < 2`, retaining the original equation at totals at least two. The same
rule applies to the original 100-step initialization and deterministic insurance
scenarios, including fractional adult counts. Other processes, forcings,
parameters, removal timing, observation records, q values, and event-keyed random
streams remain fixed. This is a necessary breeder-count guard, not an explicit
sex, mate-encounter or spatial breeding model.

Each of the 517 frozen sources was evaluated with its 1,024 archived validation
seed pairs: **529,408 new historical calls**. These are paired replays, not fresh
independent validation of the selected sources. The original kernel and archives
remain unchanged. The eight-worker run and analysis completed on deep-server;
no further simulations followed.

Verification checked 1,555 production output hashes, 85 source/input bindings,
parameter identities, all seed pairs and **20,117,504 annual records**. Every
active guard had zero recruitment mean and zero realised recruits. Recorded
stage/compartment totals and terminal survival reconcile exactly. The archives
contain 5,615,255 active-guard history-years; many occur after extinction, so this
count is not a measure of singleton rescue dependence. There are 159 unweighted
terminal lake-only histories, retained as survivors under the primary total-range
endpoint. Local retrieval hashes also match the completed archives.

The execution envelope remains 529,408 historical calls plus at most 15,000
auxiliary executions; 3,347 auxiliary executions were conservatively allocated,
including production initialization and insurance evaluations. The conservative
whole-programme ceiling remains **5,320,368**, below 7.1 million. No adaptive
replication, refitting, additional arm, commit, push or publication occurred.

## Compatibility and the viability screen

The permissive primary slice remains early-pattern lower95 at least 0.50,
joint-weight lower95 at least 1e-6, and joint-weight Monte Carlo
qualification applied to each kernel's own weights. Every comparison is within a fixed q, observation law and
configuration. The full original threshold grid remains in the transition table.

| Restriction | Originally qualified profiles | Still qualified |
|---|---:|---:|
| R_H: habitat only | 543 | 119 |
| R_T: habitat and trout allowed, climbing effects zero | 104 | 9 |
| R_C: habitat and climbing allowed, trout effects zero | 606 | 360 |
| R_J: both biotic effects allowed | 274 | 112 |
| Total | 1,527 | 600 |

This table is an inventory of the selected profiles. Its unequal denominators,
source-selection history and repeated q/law representations preclude reading
retention proportions as relative support for mechanisms.

Across all primary profiles of the fixed 517-source set, there are **609** new
qualified-compatible profiles: the 600 retained profiles plus nine that were not
originally qualified under that particular q/law. All occur at the already frozen
parameter points. This is not a search for new witnesses.

**108 sources fail the amended viability screen.** Every failure is specifically
Oberon total abundance in 2012 failing to exceed the 34 founders. All 517 sources
still pass annual Oberon persistence, juveniles by 1997, and all three
Strathgordon criteria. Thus these are failures of the prescribed deterministic
net-growth test; they do not demonstrate founder extinction or general biological
non-viability. All 1,024 histories from each failed source were retained, and
its own joint weight was set to zero without replacement.

The initialized states differ between kernels for 22 of the 24 qualification
sources. Their guard first activates at burn-in step two and then on alternating
steps (50 of 100). The other two sources retain exactly matching initialization;
forcing matches all 24. This is an observed effect of the approved coarse
closure, not an arithmetic or stream error. It exposes sensitivity to the retained
initialization phase. The 24-source check is not a claim that the same proportion
applies to all 517 sources, and this experiment does not separate initialization,
insurance-screen and historical-transition contributions factorially.

## Survival: paired process change and own-weight rescoring

These remain separate estimands. Among the original 1,527 qualified profiles,
**1,073 paired contrasts are defined and 454 are undefined** because an amended
invalid-removal history has finite original log support. The latter profiles
were not rescued by dropping histories or renormalising their original weights.
Across all 6,204 evaluated source/q/law profiles, the corresponding counts are
4,368 defined and 1,836 undefined.

Among the 1,073 defined original-profile contrasts, 451 decrease, five increase
and 617 are exactly zero. Differences span -0.6059 to +0.0372. Direction counts
include estimates close to zero and are not significance counts. Two previously
identified base sources illustrate material decreases **at q=0.02 under shared
survey-quality detection**, where both were originally qualified profiles:

| Fixed source | Original weighted survival | Guarded survival under original weights | Paired difference (MC SE) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| R_C, seed 20260918, w1 s0011 | 0.5111 | 0.2199 | -0.2912 (0.0353) |
| R_J, seed 20260919, w3 s0082 | 0.5281 | 0.2045 | -0.3236 (0.0387) |

Neither profile remains qualified-compatible under its amended own weights.
R_C fails the early-pattern criterion; R_J is unresolved because joint-weight
Monte Carlo qualification fails. Their own-weight survival estimates are 0.2031
(MC SE 0.0481) and 0.0946 (0.0294), respectively, and must not be presented as
qualified surviving witnesses. The paired estimates describe changes under the
old factual weights; they do not determine the own-weight results by subtraction.

The amended **base configuration, q=0.005, homogeneous-detection** slice retains:

| Restriction | Compatible source profiles | Own-weight terminal survival range | K_R range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| R_H | 6 | 1.000–1.000 | 20.59–47.40 |
| R_T | 1 | 1.000–1.000 | 57.57–57.57 |
| R_C | 17 | 0.703–1.000 | 47.52–140.01 |
| R_J | 6 | 1.000–1.000 | 58.73–183.43 |

All 30 profiles pass the declared survivor-tail precision checks as well as
joint-weight qualification. These ranges compare fixed parameter points, not
mechanism-averaged probabilities. An estimated value of 1.000 is a finite-sample
weighted outcome, not certainty about persistence.

Across all configurations and primary profiles, **574 of the 609 compatible
profiles also pass survivor-tail qualification**. Some strikingly low survival
estimates do not. For example, at q=0.005 homogeneous detection, the minimum
compatible estimate is 0.1101 (MC SE 0.0222), from the minimum-dated-adult-first
R_C source `20260918|w3|s0106`; its survivor ESS is only 22.8 and survivor-weight
relative error 0.207. The minimum in that q/law among profiles passing both
qualifications is 0.3245. The sole primary compatible profile at q=0.2 has a
survivor ESS of about 1.65; its very small survival point estimate cannot support
a precise terminal-fate conclusion. No q scenarios are ranked against each other.

Capacity dependence also remains visible: **23 of the 609 primary compatible
profiles, from six sources, have K_R below 20**. They belong to R_C (13 profiles)
and R_J (10). None of the retained R_H or R_T examples depends on K_R below 20.
This does not validate their abundance scale, encounter assumptions or empirical
survey coverage; it addresses the declared small-capacity reporting stratum only.

## Conditional synthesis and next decision

The test establishes sensitivity of particular witnesses to one explicit
reproductive assumption, and demonstrates that some compatible examples survive
its removal. It strengthens the case for inspecting the ecological requirements
of reconstructed histories rather than treating aggregate compatibility as
adequacy. It does not validate those histories as realistic, or make their
frequency in this selected set a likelihood ranking.

The guard also exposes a deeper dependency on stage dynamics and deterministic
initialization/screening. The next intellectual step is to decide how that
adequacy issue should constrain the scientific narrative alongside the unresolved
observation and abundance-scale assumptions. Fable's review should focus on that
interpretation and the remaining credibility of the surviving examples. A new
initialization experiment or explicit-sex kernel would require its own scoped
scientific decision; neither follows automatically from this result.
