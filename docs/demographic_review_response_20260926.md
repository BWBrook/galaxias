Inputs proposed for the next design are:

- Maximum-age scenarios of six and eight years, replacing the earlier
  six/seven suggestion. These are declared truncation assumptions; a reported
  maximum age is not proof of an absolute biological limit. Record exact age
  and census timing before making the post-1996 breeding implication.
- Effective late creek-trip totals of 2.5, 5, 10 and 20 over 1998–2002.
  Fractional trips represent coverage-equivalent exposure. Any transfer of
  Hamr's capture rate requires explicit gear, season, age and accessibility
  assumptions; effort alone does not establish equal catchability.
- Nursery allocation near one, with the old PR006 range as a sensitivity.
  Exact bounds remain to be specified. Treat stream pools as potential marginal
  nursery habitat rather than assuming all stream habitat is unsuitable.
- A reconciled dated removal ledger in the base configuration, distinguishing
  wild capture, return, captivity, offspring and translocation. Resolve overlaps
  before subtracting nominal founder totals from the historical population.
- Published Oberon and Strathgordon records as observations, with shared
  demographic quantities and separate site scales. Avoid using the same record
  to set a prior and again to screen/select histories.
- Recruitment variability in the base formulation; explicit age-0 exposure;
  post-hatch birth-year hazards; a post-inundation refuge limit; and a declared
  juvenile nursery pathway for climbing galaxias.

No new overcompensatory recruitment arm is adopted from the reported recent
Strathgordon failure: that observation alone does not identify density-driven
overcompensation. The recent record remains qualitative context in this design.
Ageing and eDNA are potential future measurements, not prerequisites. The claim
that no eDNA work has occurred should remain attributed to Chris unless checked
