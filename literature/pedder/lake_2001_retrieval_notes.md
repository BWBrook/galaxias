## Figure and methods

Figure 6 is on printed **page 94**, PDF **page 8**. It shows the mean number of
fish per site per trip for *G. pedderensis* (filled bars) and *G. parvus*
(open bars). The sampling methods are on printed **page 89**, PDF **page 3**.
They describe twelve littoral sites and four three-minute sweeps per site with
standard long-handled F.B.A. pond nets. There is also an explicitly described
November 1975 pilot at only three sites.

Two original-source inconsistencies must accompany any future digitisation:

1. The graph axis says **S.E.**, while its caption says **standard deviation**.
   Do not silently turn its bars into standard errors, standard deviations or
   a fixed observation variance. Digitised means and uncertainty-bar heights
   should remain separately labelled until their interpretation is resolved
   or carried as an explicit sensitivity.
2. The plotted time labels are **0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 11, 14 years**.
   The methods describe ten visits in December between 1975 and 1989, besides
   the November pilot. The half-year point and the pilot's participation are
   unresolved. Do not silently assign all points to annual December censuses
   with identical site counts. Preserve the plotted labels and distinguish
   known chronology from any proposed mapping.
