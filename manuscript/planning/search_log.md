# Search and verification record

Search date and cutoff: 4 October 2026, Australia/Hobart.

## Scope and approach

This is a documented narrative review for an MFR paper plan. It combines the
existing local Pedder corpus with targeted web searches, backward reference
chasing and searches for recent work. The domains covered are species history
and natural history; trout, galaxiid and habitat interactions; historical fish
ecology; occupancy and extinction inference; conservation introductions and
rescue timing; simulator assessment; genetics, refuges and eDNA monitoring.

Primary routes were publisher article pages and PDFs, author/institutional
repositories, Crossref, DataCite, official government reports and agency news.
Search results supplied discovery leads; cited scientific interpretations
were limited to the accessible source content. Retained transcriptions are
identified as such. The previous project review supplied leads but its stronger
causal conclusions were not inherited.

The detailed queries are retained in `search_log_ecology.md` and
`search_log_methods.md`. The source register records access depth and the
specific contribution/limitation of each included item. Sources were included
for a direct role in the planned argument, its scientific context or its
technical supplement. Recent papers were not included solely because they
cited Pedder. No formal screening flow, database result count or claim of
exhaustiveness is made.

## Access limits and unresolved gaps

The register distinguishes retained full transcriptions, targeted full-text
inspection, abstract-only use and metadata/context use. Availability of a full
text is not a claim that every section was critically appraised. In particular,
the methods input label `open_full_text` describes the access route; the
associated source notes and claim limits govern what was used.

No exhaustive subscription-database search or complete forward-citation export
was available. Older internal survey logs, original Hamr numeric tables and
the complete transfer ledgers were not recovered. Their absence remains
visible in the historical evidence notes. The 2024–2026 management records
extend discussion context and were not added to the completed model inputs.

The bibliography is broad enough to develop the proposed argument. Before
submission, confirm full texts for detailed claims, reconcile edition and
author metadata in Zotero, and update recent literature if the drafting
interval makes the cutoff stale.
